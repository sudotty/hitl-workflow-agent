# Showcase Plan

## Product form

A small workflow console for structured business review. The project should show that AI can prepare work, but the final decision remains easy to inspect.

## MVP screens

1. Request intake: source text and extracted fields.
2. Rule check: matched rules and notes.
3. Summary view: what the system understood.
4. Review screen: approve, reject, or ask for changes.
5. Record view: what happened and when.

## Demo flow

1. Load a sample business request.
2. Extract key fields.
3. Show rule checks.
4. Generate a clear summary.
5. Send it to review.
6. Show the final record.

## Good demo points

- The workflow is concrete.
- The system prepares work rather than hiding it.
- The reviewer can understand the recommendation.
- The final record is easy to audit.

## First-version boundary

Do not connect real business systems first. Use sample data and make the workflow clear.
